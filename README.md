# Whiteleg-shrimp-EEG-and-behavioural-analysis
Scripts for the analysis of EEG CSV files generated from LabChart, which are based on electrophysiological recordings of P. vannamei in response to different slaughter methods (cold-shock and electrical stunning with cold shock, plus electrical stunning alone), for application in a commercial setting. Data was in total power for frequencies between 0.5-32hz, and then separated out into Alpha, Beta, Delta, Theta frequencies (which can be modified in the code). CSV files were exported from Labchart and the time column was manually changed to "hh:mm:ss" for each file, and was subsampled at every second. For data subsampled at 0.5 s, CSV time data was formatted to "hh:mm:ss.0" in CSV files.
Scripts also include the plotting of behavioural data, which is based on pilot data where shrimp were observed after electrically stunning. The behaviours that were analysed and plotted were time taken to reach coordinated pleopod movement after stun (stage 2, where stage 1 is immobility), and time taken to reach a righted position (stage 3). Animals were tested over a period of 3 mins.
